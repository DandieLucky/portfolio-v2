## About the project

I designed the visual identity for Nexa Solution Group, whose logo carries the descriptor business operations consulting.

## Visual approach

I combined an N-shaped symbol with an orbit line and pixel elements. I used blue gradients for the symbol, then prepared a company wordmark and a standalone icon.
