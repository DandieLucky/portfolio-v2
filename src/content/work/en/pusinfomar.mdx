## About the project

This project covers video editing for the Indonesian National Armed Forces’ Maritime Information Centre (Pusinfomar TNI). The material shows the editing process in Adobe Premiere Pro.

## My role

I edit and sequence the video material, arrange on-screen text, and combine visual and audio elements on the timeline.

## Project material

The two recordings below show work on the editing timeline. The excerpts include the Pusinfomar identity, information material, and sequences of video clips.
