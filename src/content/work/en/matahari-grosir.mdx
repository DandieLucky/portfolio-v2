## About the project

Matahari Grosir is a snack wholesale business. I have worked on its promotional social media content part-time since May 2025.

## My role

My work covers feed designs, carousels and stories, short videos, captions, content calendars, and publishing schedules, with a consistent visual style across the material.

## Project material

I prepared chip flavor promotions and Snackpedia packaging catalogs for Matahari Grosir content. The designs combine product names, flavor and pack size information, and product and packaging compositions.
